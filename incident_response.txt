SEC-POL-03: CYBERSECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE PROTOCOL
1. CLASSIFICATION TIER
- Tier 1 (Low): Single system malware infection or suspected phishing email click.
- Tier 2 (Medium): Successful unauthorized access to a non-production environment.
- Tier 3 (High): Ransomware deployment, production database compromise, or active data breach.
2. REPORTING OBLIGATIONS
If an employee suspects a Tier 3 security incident, they must immediately call the internal emergency hotline at extension 9119 or send an urgent Slack message to the channel #sec-ops-alert. Do not attempt to clean or turn off the machine manually, as this destroys forensic evidence.
3. ISOLATION STEP
For suspected ransomware or malware escalation, the employee must disconnect the network cable or shut off the Wi-Fi card immediately to isolate the host from the corporate local area network (LAN).

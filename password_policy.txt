SEC-POL-01: CORPORATE PASSWORD AND AUTHENTICATION STANDARD
1. COMPLEXITY AND LENGTH
All employee accounts must utilize passwords with a minimum length of 14 characters. Passwords must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one numeric digit, and one special character (e.g., !, @, #, $).
2. ROTATION AND LIFESPAN
Passwords for standard employee workstations expire automatically every 90 days. System administrator account passwords expire every 45 days. Reusing any of the last 5 previous passwords is strictly prohibited.
3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all corporate portal entry points. Employees must use the Okta Verify mobile application. Hardware tokens (YubiKeys) are reserved exclusively for the infrastructure engineering team.
